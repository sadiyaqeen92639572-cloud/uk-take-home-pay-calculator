Frequently Asked Questions

How much is £38,000 after tax in the UK?
On a £38,000 salary in England, Wales or Northern Ireland, take-home pay is £30,880 a year — £2,573 a month, or £594 a week — after £5,086 Income Tax and £2,034 National Insurance, 2026/27 rates.
Is £38,000 a good salary in the UK?
This is £1,039 (3%) below the UK full-time median salary of £39,039 (ONS, April 2025). Whether it feels "good" also depends heavily on where you live — the same salary stretches much further outside London and the South East.
What tax band is £38,000 in?
In England, Wales and Northern Ireland, £38,000 falls in the Basic rate (20.0% marginal rate). In Scotland, it falls in the Intermediate rate (21.0% marginal rate) under Scotland's separate Income Tax bands.
Is take-home pay on £38,000 different in Scotland?
Yes — Scotland's Income Tax bands differ from the rest of the UK. On £38,000, Scottish take-home pay is £30,835 a year, compared to £30,880 in England, Wales and Northern Ireland — a difference of £45.
