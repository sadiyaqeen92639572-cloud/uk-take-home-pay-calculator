Frequently Asked Questions

How much is £26.00 an hour annually in the UK?
£26.00 an hour is £50,700 a year on a standard 37.5-hour working week (52 weeks), or £54,080 a year on a 40-hour week. Take-home pay after Income Tax and National Insurance is £39,963 a year (37.5h) — £3,330 a month.
Is £26.00 an hour good pay in the UK?
This is £13.29 (105%) above the 2026/27 National Living Wage of £12.71. £26.00 an hour is typical for skilled trades, teaching assistants, junior professional and technical roles.
Does £26.00 an hour change if I work 40 hours instead of 37.5?
Yes — at 40 hours a week your gross annual salary is £54,080 instead of £50,700, a difference of £3,380 a year, because you're simply working more paid hours.
What tax do I pay on £26.00 an hour?
On the 37.5-hour-week figure of £50,700, you'd pay £7,712 Income Tax and £3,025 National Insurance a year, 2026/27 rates, England/Wales/NI — leaving £39,963 take-home. Scotland uses different Income Tax bands; see the Scotland Salary Calculator.
