Frequently Asked Questions

What were the Income Tax rates in 2023/24?
England, Wales and Northern Ireland used the same bands as 2026/27: £12,570 tax-free Personal Allowance, then 20% up to £50,270, 40% up to £125,140, and 45% above — these bands have been frozen since 2021/22. Scotland used its own bands (see table above).
What was the National Insurance rate in 2023/24?
National Insurance changed mid-year: 12% from April 2023, cut to 10% from 6 January 2024. This calculator uses a blended annual rate of 11.5% (9 months at 12% + 3 months at 10%) — your actual in-year deduction varied by pay period.
Why would I need 2023/24 tax rates instead of the current year?
Common reasons: checking an old payslip or P60 for accuracy, working out backdated pay or a tribunal settlement, comparing your pay rise year-on-year, or verifying HMRC correspondence about a previous tax year.
Can I still file or amend a 2023/24 Self Assessment return?
HMRC generally allows amendments to a Self Assessment return up to 12 months after the filing deadline for that year — check gov.uk/self-assessment-tax-returns/corrections for current rules, as deadlines vary by circumstance.
