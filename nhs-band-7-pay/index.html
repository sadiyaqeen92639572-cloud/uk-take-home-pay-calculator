NHS Band 7 Pay in 2025/26

Under the 2025/26 Agenda for Change scale, Band 7 ran from £47,810 to £54,710, before the 3.3% uplift on 1 April 2026 raised it to £49,387–£56,515 for 2026/27. An old payslip or P60 from the 2025/26 tax year will show the lower figure. For the full structure across every band and recent years, see the NHS pay bands overview.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does NHS Band 7 pay in 2026/27?
NHS Band 7 ranges from £49,387 a year at the entry point to £56,515 at the top of the band, under the 2026/27 Agenda for Change pay scale (3.3% uplift from 1 April 2026).
What is the take-home pay for NHS Band 7?
At the top of Band 7 (£56,515 gross), take-home pay is £43,336 a year (£3,611 a month) in England, Wales and Northern Ireland, after £10,038 Income Tax and £3,141 National Insurance. In Scotland, take-home is £41,656 due to different Income Tax bands.
What was NHS Band 7 pay in 2025/26?
Under the 2025/26 Agenda for Change scale, Band 7 ran from £47,810 to £54,710, before the 3.3% uplift on 1 April 2026 raised it to £49,387–£56,515 for 2026/27.
What roles are in NHS Band 7?
Advanced nurse practitioners, team leaders, senior specialist staff. Exact job titles vary by trust and role profile — check your trust's job description against the NHS Job Evaluation Scheme for a definitive banding.
Does NHS Band 7 pay include unsocial hours or overtime?
No — these figures are basic Agenda for Change pay only. Unsocial hours enhancements, overtime, high-cost area supplements (London weighting) and on-call payments are calculated separately and added on top of basic pay.
